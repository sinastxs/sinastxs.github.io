Privacy Policy for DealList

Last updated: August 18, 2026

DealList ("we", "our", or "us") is operated by DEALLIST SOFTWARE LTD. This Privacy Policy explains how the DealList mobile application (the "Service") handles data.

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect, store, or transmit personal shopping-list content to our own servers.

  • User-generated content: Shopping lists, favorites, price corrections, and custom notes are stored locally on your device. We do not have access to this local content.
  • No account required: You do not need to register an account or provide an email address, phone number, or name to use DealList.

2. Third-Party Services and Cloud Data

DealList downloads grocery flyer data and related app data from cloud services so the app can show current local offers.

  • Google Firebase / Firestore: The app communicates with Firebase/Firestore to download grocery data. Google Play Services and Firebase may process automated log data, performance metrics, or basic device identifiers for stability and network synchronization.
  • Google AdMob: The free version of DealList may display ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may process data according to Google's advertising and privacy policies.
  • RevenueCat and app stores: Optional purchases, subscriptions, or ad-removal entitlement checks may be handled by RevenueCat, Google Play, or Apple, depending on your platform.
  • Translation services: When translation is used, text required for translation may be sent to the relevant translation service.

We encourage you to review these third-party policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy and Security
  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy

3. Data Storage and Security

Your personal app data, such as shopping lists and preferences, is stored locally on your device. If you delete DealList or clear its app data, locally stored lists and preferences may be permanently removed.

4. Children's Privacy

DealList is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

6. Contact Us

For privacy questions, support, or bug reports, contact us at info@deallist.ca.
